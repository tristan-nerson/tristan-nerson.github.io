I am a doctoral researcher at EPFL's [Laboratory of Wave Engineering](https://www.epfl.ch/labs/lwe/), working with Prof. Romain Fleury. I study how waves exchange momentum with matter and how to shape acoustic and electromagnetic fields to control forces and torques.

My work combines theory, computation, and experiments in complex scattering environments. I am particularly interested in wavefront shaping, multiobjective control, and micromanipulation that adapts to its surroundings without a detailed model.
