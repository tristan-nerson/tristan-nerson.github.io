I study scattering and wave–matter interactions in acoustics, electromagnetism, and water waves. My research asks how waves transfer momentum to objects, and how the incident field and surrounding environment can be used to control that transfer.

My current interests include radiation forces and torques, wavefront shaping in complex media, simultaneous control of multiple objects, and adaptive micromanipulation informed by measurements. I use theoretical, numerical, and experimental approaches.

Some of my work also explores more broadly how an environment changes the response of a radiating system. Details of ongoing projects will be added when they are public.
